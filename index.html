Privacy Policy
Last updated: 12 November 2026

Overview

Kilo is a personal workout tracking app. This policy explains what data the app keeps, where it is stored, and the few moments when data can leave your phone, each of them started by you.

Everything Kilo records stays on your phone. The app has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no internet permission. We never see your data and cannot sell or share it.

Data stored on your device

Kilo keeps the following in a local database and in app settings on your phone:

  • Workout sessions – name, date, duration, notes, effort rating
  • Exercises and sets – name, muscle group, weight, reps, warm-up flag, notes
  • Workout templates, routines and workout types
  • Your own exercises added to the library
  • Injury notes and which exercises to avoid
  • AI review replies you paste back, and the coaching notes derived from them
  • Progression settings and personal records computed from your sessions
  • App preferences – theme, units, rest timer, reminders

None of this is transmitted by the app. It leaves your phone only through the actions described below.

When data leaves your phone (only when you choose)

  • AI review. When you ask for a review, Kilo writes a text summary of the workouts being reviewed (exercises, sets, your notes, and optionally your active injuries and the previous review's advice) and hands it to an app you pick in the share sheet, such as an AI chat app. Kilo does not send anything itself. What that app does with the text is governed by its own privacy policy. You can also copy the text to the clipboard. The reply you paste back is stored on your phone.
  • Export data. Creates a CSV file of your sessions and opens the share sheet so you can save or send it where you want.
  • Send feedback. Opens your email app with a message to support.kiloapp@gmail.com. The message is prefilled with the app version and your Android version so we can help; everything else is what you type.
  • Phone backup. If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android includes Kilo's database and settings in that backup, like any other app. It is encrypted and managed by Google under your Google account settings. A workout in progress is not backed up.

Permissions

  • Notifications – rest timer alerts and optional workout reminders. You can turn both off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Alarms and reminders – lets the rest timer end exactly on time. If you do not allow it, the timer still works but may be a little late when the screen is off.
  • Foreground service – keeps the rest countdown running and visible on the lock screen while a rest is in progress. It stops as soon as the rest ends.
  • Run at startup – reschedules workout reminders after a restart. No data is read or sent.
  • Vibration and wake lock – for the end-of-rest alert and an accurate countdown.

Kilo does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or any health sensor.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it. Sessions you delete go to Recently deleted for 7 days, where you can restore them, and are then removed permanently.

Settings → Delete all data erases every session, template, routine, exercise and injury from the phone. Uninstalling Kilo removes everything the app stored. A copy may remain in your phone's Google backup until that backup is replaced or you turn backup off in your Google account settings.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot delete it on your behalf.

Children

Kilo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
