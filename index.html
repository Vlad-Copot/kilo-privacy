Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 2025

Overview

Kilo is a personal workout tracking app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app uses, how it is stored, and when it leaves your device.

All workout data is stored locally on your device. Kilo does not have servers, does not create user accounts, and does not sell or share your data with third parties.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in a SQLite database on your device and never transmitted unless you explicitly use the AI Coach feature:

  • Workout sessions — name, date, notes, effort rating
  • Exercises — name, muscle group, sets, reps, weight
  • Workout templates
  • Injury logs
  • App preferences — theme, units, notification settings

This data never leaves your device except as described in the AI Coach section below.

AI Coach — Google Gemini API

Kilo includes an optional AI Coach feature powered by Google Gemini. When you tap "Analyse with AI", a summary of your recent workout data is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate personalised coaching insights.

The data sent includes:

  • Recent session names, dates, and volume
  • Weekly muscle group volume totals
  • Progression suggestions generated on-device

This data is processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. It is not linked to your identity and contains no personal identifiers.

The AI Coach feature is rate-limited to once per 24 hours. You can choose never to use it — all other app features work entirely offline.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

  • Notifications — used for optional workout reminders. You can disable this at any time in Settings.
  • Receive boot completed — used to reschedule workout reminders after device restart. No data is collected.
  • Internet — used only when the AI Coach feature is actively triggered by you.

Data retention & deletion

All data is stored on your device. To delete all app data, uninstall Kilo from your device. This permanently removes all workout history, templates, and preferences.

We have no ability to delete data on your behalf as we do not have access to it.

Children

Kilo is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
